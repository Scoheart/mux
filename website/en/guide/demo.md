## In the video

- **Models**: central Model cards and a Provider source.
- **MCPs**: tool connections and configuration in one library.
- **Skills**: source navigation, names, and three-line card descriptions.
- **Provider editing**: editable portal, model-list URL, and protocol Paths.
- **Agent picker**: card entries for CLI, Desktop, IDE, and Plugin products.

Recorded in the version- and signature-verified 1.10.0 app with public sample assets. Example Model IDs and endpoints are illustrative, not callable services; no model requests are sent. Choose Chinese or English captions in the player or download the MP4.
